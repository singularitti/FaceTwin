# WFLW photograph attribution

WFLW test images and annotations: Wu et al., *Look at Boundary: A Boundary-Aware Face Alignment Algorithm*, CVPR 2018. Geometry overlays and evaluation results were produced by FaceTwin.

- Author project: https://github.com/wywu/LAB
- Paper: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Wu_Look_at_Boundary_CVPR_2018_paper.html
- Annotation conversion: https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmpose/blob/main/docs/en/dataset_zoo/2d_face_keypoint.md

These ten PNG files are target crops from the author's linked WFLW image archive, used to illustrate the reported geometry measurements. Cropping preserves original pixel resolution and existing watermarks. Their assigned WFLW record IDs and source dataset filenames are provided in `data/results.json`.

The photographs belong to their original rights holders. They are separate from FaceTwin's software license and are not offered under an open-image license. The dataset documentation requests research citation; its historical author page contains an academic-use designation but does not provide an explicit general photograph-republication license. This attribution must not be interpreted as such a license.
